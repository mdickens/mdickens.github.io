Blog: 06-08-2025: Behind the Clouds: Developing a Simple Roblox Obby

We developed an obby, which is short for obstacle course, published it on Roblox. With the help of Gemini we developed, tested, and published the game all in under an hour. We used Lua for the logic. My daughter's Roblox skills were vital during the testing phase to jump from one platform to another. Then for publishing the video below we used HHandbrake to convert the video file from .mov to .mp4 format. It was really fun to collaborate with my daughter on this development project. Thanks, Gemini!

A

Our Cloud Ascent Obby leverages the Roblox Engine and its Luau scripting language. Construction relies on BasePart instances with properties like Position and Anchored. Dynamic obstacles utilize Luau scripts attached to parts, employing event listeners like Touched and manipulating properties like Transparency and CanCollide. Checkpoints manage Player.RespawnLocation, potentially integrating Player.TeamColor for non-neutral spawns. Victory is celebrated client-side with ScreenGui manipulation, ParticleEmitter activation, and Sound playback, all orchestrated by server-side Luau within the victory trigger.
