Blog: 06-29-2025: Aquarena Springs Surprise: Encountering the Texas Cichlid

During our recent visit to Aquarena Springs in San Marcos, Texas, we had the pleasure of viewing this fish in an aquarium, a captivating specimen. We went to Meadows Center for Water and the Environment experience, specifically for the The Glass-Bottom Boats Tour. The fish featured is most likely a Herichthys cyanoguttatus, commonly known as the Texas Cichlid. This species is native to the Rio Grande basin in Texas and Mexico.

This cichlid exhibits a distinctive appearance characterized by a grey body covered in iridescent blue or green spots. Males often develop a nuchal hump, adding to their robust build. Its notable features include a stout body, thick lips, and a rounded head. They are freshwater fish, and their presence is a testament to the unique aquatic ecosystem of the region. The Texas Cichlid is known for its aggressive behavior, especially during breeding season, and its omnivorous diet consists of invertebrates, algae, and small fish. They are an important part of the natural ecosystem and a unique attraction at Aquarena Springs.
