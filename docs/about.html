Professional Bio

Marc Dickenson is a Senior Software Development Manager responsible for software development of an asset management product. He is passionate about being a great manager, mentor, and coach. His background is as a software developer with over two decades of experience in the software development lifecycle including: planning, analysis, design, implementing, testing and maintaining software. He uses a data-driven approach by collecting and analyzing data to measure key performance indicators and improve engineering efficiency. He is also the author of thirty patents.
