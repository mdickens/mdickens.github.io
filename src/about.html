Professional Bio

As a Senior Software Development Manager at IBM, Marc Dickenson leads software development for the Maximo product. He brings over twenty years of hands-on experience spanning the full software development lifecycle, from initial concept to ongoing maintenance. Marc is a strong advocate for data-driven decision-making, leveraging analytics to boost engineering efficiency. Beyond his technical prowess, he's deeply committed to being a top-tier manager, mentor, and coach. Marc's innovative spirit is evident in his thirty authored patents.
